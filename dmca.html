DMCA & Copyright Compliance Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2026 • Official Legal Statement

TheBhom (accessible at https://www.thebhom.in) respects the intellectual property rights of creators, artists, developers, authors, and copyright holders worldwide. We expect all visitors, users, and API consumers of our services to do the same.

It is our policy to respond with expeditious diligence to clear notices of alleged copyright infringement that comply with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act of 1998 (17 U.S.C. § 512, "DMCA"), the Indian Copyright Act, 1957 (as amended), and the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.

⚡ Technical Architecture & Intermediary Conduit Declaration

1. Zero Media Hosting: TheBhom does not host, clone, mirror, transcode, or permanently store any copyrighted video files, proprietary audio streams, or media archives on its web servers or Cloudflare edge nodes.

2. Client-Side Browser Execution: The tools provided on this website (such as the Universal Video Downloader, Audio Extractor, and Image Converter) operate as automated, transient client-side technical intermediaries. All data streams flow directly between the end-user's client browser and the third-party public content delivery network (CDN).

3. Non-Indexation: TheBhom does not maintain a public search index, database, or library of downloaded video URLs or unauthorized media files.

1. Fair Use & Acceptable Educational Utility Policy

Under Section 107 of the United States Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 107) and Section 52 of the Indian Copyright Act, 1957, the doctrine of "Fair Dealing" and "Fair Use" recognizes that the reproduction of certain copyrighted material is permissible without the copyright holder's permission for purposes such as:

  • Personal Archival & Offline Backup: Backing up user-owned original videos, licensed content, or private family records.
  • Teaching, Scholarship & Research: Utilizing short video excerpts, clips, or soundbites for classroom lectures, academic analysis, or critique.
  • News Reporting & Commentary: Referencing public announcements, press releases, or documentary footage for transformative journalistic critique.
  • Creative Commons & Public Domain: Processing media distributed under Creative Commons licenses (CC-BY, CC0) or works that have lapsed into the public domain.

🚫 Strictly Prohibited Activities

Users are expressly forbidden from utilizing TheBhom utilities to:

  • Download, reproduce, or distribute DRM-protected (Digital Rights Management) content (such as Netflix, Amazon Prime, Disney+, Hulu, or Spotify encrypted streams).
  • Bypass technological protection measures (TPMs) in violation of 17 U.S.C. § 1201.
  • Commercialize, sell, re-broadcast, or monetize any third-party copyrighted work without explicit written license from the copyright proprietor.

3. Formal DMCA Notice Requirements

If you are a copyright owner, or authorized to act on behalf of one, and you believe that your copyrighted work is being infringed through any link, page, or service on TheBhom, please provide a written notification containing all of the following statutory elements:

  1. Physical or Electronic Signature: A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.
  2. Identification of the Copyrighted Work: Precise description of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, or, if multiple works at a single online site are covered by a single notification, a representative list of such works (including registration numbers, if applicable).
  3. Identification of the Infringing Material: Clear identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing or to be the subject of infringing activity and that is to be removed or access to which is to be disabled, including the exact URL(s) on https://www.thebhom.in.
  4. Contact Information of Complaining Party: Sufficient information to permit TheBhom to contact you, such as your legal name, physical mailing address, telephone number, and official corporate email address.
  5. Statement of Good Faith Belief: A statement that you have a "good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law."
  6. Statement Under Penalty of Perjury: A statement that "the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that the complaining party is authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed."

Please deliver your formal notification to our designated agent at legal@thebhom.in with the subject line "DMCA Takedown Notice - [Your Name / Entity]".

4. Expedited Review & Remediation Process

Upon receipt of a bona fide, complete infringement notice meeting the statutory criteria outlined above, TheBhom will take the following immediate actions:

  • Investigate the specific URL, deal link, guide reference, or API pathway within 24 to 48 business hours.
  • Disable access to, remove, or permanently blacklist any offending link, URL pattern, or third-party reference from our servers and routing tables.
  • Notify the content poster or user (where contact information is technically available) of the takedown action taken.

5. Counter-Notification Procedure

If you believe that your link, content, or material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification, you may submit a formal Counter-Notification to our Designated Agent pursuant to Sections 512(g)(2) and (3) of the DMCA. Your counter-notification must be in writing and include:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. Identification of the material that has been removed or to which access has been disabled and the location at which the material appeared before it was removed or disabled.
  3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification of the material.
  4. Your name, address, and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court for the judicial district in which the address is located (or if outside the United States, to any judicial district in which the service provider may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who provided notification under subsection (c)(1)(C) or an agent of such person.

Upon receipt of a valid counter-notification, TheBhom will promptly provide the person who submitted the original notice with a copy of the counter-notification and inform that person that TheBhom will replace the removed material or cease disabling access to it within 10 to 14 business days, unless our agent receives notice from the original complainant that an action has been filed seeking a court order.
