Whether you are downloading a podcast, an acoustic music performance, or a background soundscape for video editing, the quality of your audio file is determined by one critical specification: Bitrate (measured in kbps).

128kbps vs 192kbps vs 320kbps: What is the Real Difference?

Bitrate Audio Quality File Size (4 min song) Recommended Listening Device
128 kbps Basic / Compressed ~3.8 MB Basic smartphone speaker
192 kbps Standard Quality ~5.7 MB Budget TWS earphones
320 kbps High-Fidelity Studio ~9.6 MB Car stereos, studio monitors, audiophile headphones

At 128kbps, high frequencies (cymbals, guitar strings, and vocal sibilance) are aggressively compressed and filtered out to save bandwidth. On decent car speakers or over-ear headphones, 128kbps sounds muffled and "flat." At 320kbps Constant Bitrate (CBR), the full 20Hz - 20,000Hz human hearing range is preserved with wide stereo separation.
