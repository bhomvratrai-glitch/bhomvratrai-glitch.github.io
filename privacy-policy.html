Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

Welcome to TheBhom (accessible at thebhom.in and its subdomains). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy and providing transparent information regarding how data is processed when using our website, video tools, and media players.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require user account registration for our public video downloading, previewing, and streaming utilities. We do not store or collect personal names, home addresses, or payment card details for free utility tools. Automatic diagnostic logs may temporarily register IP addresses, browser types, and requested URLs strictly for server maintenance, security, and rate-limiting purposes.

2. Google AdSense and DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to thebhom.in and other sites on the internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

3. Log Files

Like many standard web servers, TheBhom utilizes standard log files. The information inside the log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

4. Third-Party Links & Fair Use

Our tools allow users to process public video URLs for personal playback, archiving, and educational fair use. We do not host copyrighted video files on our primary servers; all media requests are fetched directly through public video streams upon user demand.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us at: contact@thebhom.in.
