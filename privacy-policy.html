Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2026 • Official Data Governance Statement

Welcome to TheBhom (accessible at https://www.thebhom.in and its affiliated subdomains). We are deeply committed to respecting your privacy, protecting your personal data, and maintaining the highest standards of transparency regarding our data handling, advertising technologies, and web utility operations.

This Privacy Policy explains in detail how we collect, use, store, process, and disclose information when you visit our website, read our consumer guides, interact with verified shopping deals, or utilize our browser-based video downloaders and media utilities.

1. The Types of Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver fast, functional, and secure services:

A. Information You Provide Voluntarily

  • Correspondence Data: When you contact our support desk, submit an editorial query, send a feedback form, or file a DMCA notice, you provide your name, email address, and the specific contents of your message.
  • Search & Filter Queries: Text strings entered into our deal search bars or guide search forms are processed entirely in memory or via client-side storage to deliver immediate results.

B. Automated Technical & Log Data

Like almost all modern internet web applications, when you visit TheBhom, our web servers and edge nodes automatically log standard technical data:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) Address: Used temporarily for fraud prevention, server DDoS mitigation, geolocation filtering (country-level), and rate limiting.
  • Device & Browser Metadata: Browser type, operating system, device screen resolution, referring/exit URLs, date and time stamps, and preferred language.
  • Performance Telemetry: Page response times, download errors, and asset load times to optimize speed across mobile and desktop devices.

🔒 Browser-First Processing Commitment

TheBhom does not require mandatory account registration to browse deals or use our video download tools. We do not collect credit card numbers, banking passwords, or government identification IDs for free utilities. Furthermore, video parsing is conducted client-side via public CDN streams; media files are never saved or stored on our servers.

5. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA & CPRA) Compliance

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have specific privacy rights regarding their personal information:

  • Right to Know: You have the right to request information about the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you over the past 12 months.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request the deletion of any personal information collected from you, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you, deny services, charge different prices, or provide a different quality of service for exercising your CCPA rights.
  • "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information": TheBhom does NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for monetary consideration. We do allow third-party advertising partners (like Google) to collect technical cookie identifiers for contextual advertising as disclosed herein.

To exercise any of your California privacy rights, please email us at privacy@thebhom.in with the subject line "CCPA Privacy Request".

6. European Economic Area (EEA) & UK GDPR Compliance

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, our processing of your personal data is governed by the General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR) and UK Data Protection Act 2018.

Legal Bases for Processing

  • Legitimate Interests: Operating and securing our platform, optimizing website performance, preventing malicious automated abuse, and maintaining editorial integrity.
  • Consent: Serving personalized third-party advertisements and processing optional communications where explicit consent is requested.
  • Legal Obligation: Complying with statutory copyright notices (DMCA), tax, or regulatory requirements.

Your GDPR Rights

You have the right to access, rectify, restrict processing, request erasure (Right to be Forgotten), data portability, and object to processing of your personal data. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local European Data Protection Authority (DPA).

7. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

TheBhom does not knowingly target, solicit, or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain EU jurisdictions). All our web utilities, articles, and deal collections are intended for general adult audiences and young adult consumers.

If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately at privacy@thebhom.in, and we will take immediate steps to delete such data from our records.

8. Data Security and Retention

We deploy robust industry-standard technical safeguards, including HTTPS encryption across all subdomains (TLS 1.3), Cloudflare edge security barriers, strict Content Security Policies, and automated vulnerability scanning. Diagnostic server log files are automatically purged or rotated every 30 to 90 days.
